Why the numbers hold up

The recommendations are only as good as the data behind them. Here's how that stays accurate.

Refreshed automatically

Prices and stock update as players browse overseas shops, not on a fixed schedule — so recently-visited countries stay current.

Built from real shopping, not guesses

Contributions come from actual store visits in the background, not manual entry, so there's nothing for anyone to fill in or forget.

Your settings, applied everywhere

Capacity, tax, and flight type are applied consistently across the planner and the country helper, so the two never disagree.
